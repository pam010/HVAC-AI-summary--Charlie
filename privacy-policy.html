HVAC AI LEAD RECOVERY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how HVAC AI Lead Recovery (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects and uses information submitted through our call-recovery forms and related communications.

Information we collect

Depending on the request, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, service ZIP code, HVAC problem description, preferred callback window, and photos or files you choose to upload.

How we use information

We use this information to record your service request, share it with the participating HVAC service business, send a request-confirmation or callback message, and help the business respond to you. We do not use submitted information to make automated service, pricing, or eligibility decisions.

Sharing and service providers

Information may be stored and processed by the CRM, messaging, form, hosting, and automation providers used to operate this service. We share information with the HVAC business responsible for responding to your request. We do not sell personal information.

Photos and files

Uploaded photos are optional unless a form says otherwise. They are used only to help the service business understand the reported issue and may be viewed by its authorized team members.

Text messages

If you provide consent, we may send non-marketing messages about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Retention and security

We retain request information only as needed for service coordination, records, dispute handling, and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may ask the participating service business about your submitted information or request correction where applicable. To ask a privacy question, use the contact method provided by the business that sent you the form or message.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
